01 Mission & vision

A culture of active knowledge transfer.

The Seniors Teach, Juniors Reach initiative at P.V.K.N. Government Degree College (Autonomous), Chittoor, cultivates a peer-to-peer ecosystem where senior students who have mastered essential practical technologies, software tools, and project workflows mentor junior peers in dedicated, hands-on lab sessions.

It is not a lecture. It is a workshop, a study circle, and a launching pad — all rolled into two concentrated days.

02 Invitation

Every stream is invited.

Whether you are from Computer Science, Commerce, Life Sciences, or Humanities — digital and practical literacy belongs to every career path.

Two-day architecture

A workshop that earns its hours.

Every block of time is engineered. Fundamentals on day one, real projects on day two. No filler, no padding — just the practical skills you'll actually use.
